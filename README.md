## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/Objects/DNA.png" width="25" alt="" /> About

I'm a developer and Health Informatics student at the University of Basrah. I write software that sits at the intersection of **biology, data, and systems engineering** — from desktop tools and web applications to genomic data pipelines.

I care about building things that actually solve problems: healthcare management systems, bioinformatics workflows for single-cell RNA-seq, and AI-assisted tooling that makes complex data feel manageable.

Outside of code and datasets, I spend time with video editing, drone photography, and competitive gaming.
