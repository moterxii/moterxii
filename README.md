## 👤 About Me

I'm a Computer Science and **Health Informatics** student at the University of Basrah, working at the edge where software engineering meets biological data. My work focuses on building practical systems — from healthcare data platforms and desktop applications to pipelines that process genomic datasets like single-cell RNA-seq.

I care about software that solves real problems: systems that help clinicians, tools that accelerate research, and applications that turn raw data into usable insight. My current trajectory is shaped by a deep interest in **bioinformatics**, **AI-assisted development**, and **healthcare systems architecture**.

Beyond code, I spend time with video editing, drone photography, and gaming — hobbies that keep my creative and analytical sides balanced.
